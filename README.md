# DAF22-Main-Project-Employee-compensation-Intelligence
PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Domain:** Experience-Based Compensation Analytics

**Objective:** To analyze employee experience, departmental distribution, and salary structure in order to understand how experience levels influence compensation patterns across the organization.

**Business Problem:** The organization maintains employee records containing experience, department, and salary details, but lacks clear insight into whether compensation is consistently aligned with experience across different departments. The problem is to assess the relationship between employee experience and salary and identify departmental variations in pay structure.

**Dataset:** An employee Dataset of a specific organization containing 5000 customer records with 19 features.
